BUILT BY ATHLETES,
FOR ATHLETES

FitNation Gym was founded in 2018 out of pure frustration. Columbus lacked a true training facility—every gym was either a crowded commercial chain playing pop music or an under-equipped garage. We wanted a space where serious lifters could push their absolute limits without compromise.

Today, our 15,000 sq. ft. warehouse stands as the premier strength facility in the Midwest. Our unique selling proposition is simple: We curate an environment of extreme focus. No distractions. Just heavy iron, expert coaching, and a community that holds you accountable.

GYM FACILITIES

Everything you need to succeed, nothing you don't. We invested heavily in top-tier gear.

Weight Training Area

8 calibrated Eleiko competition racks, 10 deadlift platforms, and over 15,000 lbs of steel and bumper plates.

Cardio Zone

High-intensity focus: Concept2 Rowers, Assault AirBikes, SkiErgs, and heavy-duty curved treadmills.

CrossFit / Functional

A massive 60ft indoor turf strip with pushing sleds, plyometric boxes, kettlebells up to 150lbs, and climbing ropes.

Personal Training

Isolated functional assessment areas specifically reserved for 1-on-1 coaching safely out of heavy traffic.

Recovery & Therapy

On-site clinical space for our physical therapists to perform dry-needling, active release, and tissue work.

Locker & Changing

Immaculate bathrooms, private showers with supplied products, large day lockers, and an Infrared Sauna.
